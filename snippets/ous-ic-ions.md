**−ous / −ic metal ions:** the lower charge ends in **−ous** and the higher charge ends in **−ic**.

| Ion | Name | Ion | Name | Ion | Name |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|:-:|---|
| $\ce{Hg2^2+}$ | Mercurous | $\ce{Pb^2+}$ | Plumbous | $\ce{Sn^2+}$ | Stannous |
