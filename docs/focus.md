## How it works

Study in short, focused blocks with breaks in between (the Pomodoro method): pick a length, tap
**Start focus**, and take the break when the timer says so. The timer keeps running when you
switch pages or close the app, and a pill in the top bar shows the time left.

Finished focus sessions count toward the **study time** on the [home page](index.md), even if you
were working on paper with the screen off. While a session is running, the normal tracker pauses so
the same minutes aren't counted twice.
