## How it works

Study in short, focused blocks with breaks in between (the Pomodoro method): pick a length, tap
**Start focus**, and take the break when the timer says so. The liquid rises as your focus session
fills up, drains during a break, and goes still while the timer is paused. The timer keeps running
when you switch pages or close the app, and a pill in the top bar shows the time left.

Finished focus sessions count toward the **study time** on the [home page](index.md), even if you
were working on paper with the screen off. While a session is running, the normal tracker pauses so
the same minutes aren't counted twice.

On iPhone, the app can't ring while it's closed or the screen is locked. The timer keeps time anyway,
and finished sessions show up when you open the app again.
