## Amines (nitrogen bases)

Each amine is ammonia with one or more H's replaced by $\ce{CH3}$ groups. Nitrogen keeps its lone
pair, which is why these are bases.

| Name | Formula |
|---|---|
| Ammine (ammonia) | $\ce{NH3}$ |
| Methylamine | $\ce{CH3NH2}$ |
| Dimethylamine | $\ce{(CH3)2NH}$ |
| Trimethylamine | $\ce{(CH3)3N}$ |

Full notes: [Nitrogen Bases & Ammonia](units/02-foundations-2/nitrogen-bases-ammonia.md).

## Redox

**Lose e⁻ = oxidized, gain e⁻ = reduced.** {.star}

The species that is oxidized is the **reducing agent**; the species that is reduced is the
**oxidizing agent**. Full notes: [Oxidation Numbers](units/02-foundations-2/oxidation-numbers.md)
and [Balancing Redox Reactions](units/02-foundations-2/balancing-redox.md).
